03 — Playground

Mission control

Rockets and robots are why I got into engineering. This is a small tribute: a stylised super-heavy rocket and a catch tower with chopstick arms, built in Three.js from plain cylinders, cones, and boxes. Press launch, then drag to orbit the scene.

Mission Portfolio-1 · Flight test
Mission clock T-00:00:03

Vehicle on the pad

Drag to orbit the camera

Booster Go for launch
Altitude0.0 km Speed0 km/h
Ship Go for launch
All systems nominal. Ready when you are.
  • Built with Three.js primitives only: cylinders, cones, boxes, and a lot of easing curves. No models, no textures apart from Orbit's mission patch.
  • Sequence Countdown, liftoff, hot staging, booster flip and boostback, landing burn, then the tower's arms close for the catch.
  • Interaction Drag or swipe sideways to orbit the camera at any point. The scene only renders while on screen and respects reduced-motion settings.
